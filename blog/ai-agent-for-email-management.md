## What an AI Email Agent Actually Does

Let's be precise. An AI email agent is an automated system that connects to your inbox and takes action on your behalf based on rules you define.

This is different from:
- **Email filters** — which just move messages around
- **AI email assistants** (like Gmail's Smart Reply) — which suggest things but don't act
- **Email clients with AI features** — which still require you to open and review everything

An AI agent reads your email, makes a judgment call, and takes action. Draft a reply. Archive. Flag as urgent. Summarize and forward. Schedule a follow-up reminder. All without you touching it.

## The Three Layers of an AI Email Agent

When I teach this in my course, I break email agents into three layers:

### Layer 1: Triage
The agent reads every incoming email and categorizes it:
- Urgent (needs your attention today)
- Routine (can be handled automatically or batched)
- Noise (newsletters, notifications, FYIs — can be archived)

Most people's inboxes are 70%+ noise. Your agent handles that automatically. You never see it.

### Layer 2: Action
For routine emails, the agent takes action:
- Drafts a response using your tone and templates
- Forwards to the right person with context
- Logs the inquiry in your CRM or project tool
- Sets a reminder if follow-up is needed

You review the drafts in a batch — usually 10-15 minutes, once or twice a day.

### Layer 3: Escalation
For genuinely urgent or complex emails, the agent flags them with context:
- Why it's flagged
- Suggested next action
- Relevant background (previous thread, related contacts)

You make the call. But you make it with a briefing, not a cold read.

## The Prompt That Makes It Work

The most important part of your AI email agent isn't the tools — it's the prompt. Here's a starter template:

```
You are my email assistant. Your job is to help me process my inbox efficiently.

For each email I share with you:
1. Categorize it: Urgent / Routine / Noise
2. If Urgent: Tell me why and suggest a next action
3. If Routine: Draft a response in my voice (professional, direct, concise)
4. If Noise: Simply label it for archiving

My communication style: direct, warm, no fluff. I value brevity and clarity.
```

Customize this with your name, role, and any specific rules (e.g., "emails from [client name] are always urgent"). The more specific your prompt, the better your agent performs.

## Is This Safe? What About Privacy?

This is the question I get most from my cybersecurity colleagues — and it's a fair one.

Yes, you are connecting your email to an AI system. That means:

- Use a reputable provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, not some random free service)
- Review the data privacy terms of your automation platform
- Consider what information is in your emails and whether it warrants extra protection

For most professionals, the risk is manageable. For highly sensitive environments, there are enterprise-grade options or you can run everything locally. I cover both in the course.
